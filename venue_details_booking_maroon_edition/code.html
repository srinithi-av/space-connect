About the Space

Perched atop a historic cast-iron building in Downtown SoHo, The Glasshouse Loft & Skylight Gallery delivers unprecedented daylight illumination, bespoke architectural volumes, and museum-grade finishes. Designed specifically for commercial film & photo productions, editorial runways, luxury brand launches, and executive hospitality retreats.

The loft encompasses 3,200 square feet of column-free entertaining and production terrain. Original timber ceilings meet a 24-foot motorized north-facing skylight array, allowing total diffusion control for cinematographers and staging directors. Equipped with three-phase 200A camlock electric feeds, a dedicated 8,000 lb drive-in freight elevator opening directly into the staging floor, and a commercial-ready styling bar.
